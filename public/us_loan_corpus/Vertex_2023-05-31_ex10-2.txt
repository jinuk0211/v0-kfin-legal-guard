AMENDED
AND RESTATED INTERCREDITOR AGREEMENT

 

This
AMENDED AND RESTATED INTERCREDITOR AGREEMENT (this “
Agreement
”), originally dated as of April 1, 2022 and amended
and restated as of May 26, 2023 by and among Cantor Fitzgerald Securities, as collateral agent (in such capacity, with its successors
and assigns, the “
Term Loan Agent
”) for the Term Loan Secured Parties, Macquarie Energy North America Trading
Inc., in its capacity as intermediator under the Intermediation Facility Documents (in such capacity, with its successor and assigns,
the “
Intermediation Facility Secured Party
”), Macquarie Energy North America Trading Inc., in its capacity
as intermediator under the Renewables Intermediation Facility Documents (in such capacity, with its successor and assigns, the
“
Renewables Intermediation Facility Secured Party
”), Vertex Refining Alabama LLC, a Delaware limited liability
company (the “
Company
”), Vertex Renewables Alabama LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“
Renewables
Company
”, together with the Company, the “
Grantors
” and each, individually, a “
Grantor
”)
and each of the other companies appearing on the signature pages hereto (collectively, the “
Acknowledging Affiliates
”).

 

WHEREAS,
the Company is a borrower under the Term Loan Agreement, pursuant to which the lenders party thereto have made and/or will make
term loans to the Company on the terms and conditions set forth therein; and

 

WHEREAS,
the Company has or will become a party to the Intermediation Agreement, which provide certain arrangements for (i) the provision
of intermediation services by the Intermediation Facility Secured Party to the Company and (ii) certain other transactions between
the Intermediation Facility Secured Party and the Company related thereto; and

 

WHEREAS,
the Renewables Company has or will become a party to the Renewables Intermediation Agreement, which provide certain arrangements
for (i) the provision of intermediation services by the Renewables Intermediation Facility Secured Party to the Renewables Company
and (ii) certain other transactions between the Renewables Intermediation Facility Secured Party and the Renewables Company related
thereto; and

 

WHEREAS,
the Grantors have granted, or will grant, Liens on certain of their respective assets (i) to the Term Loan Agent pursuant to the
Term Loan Collateral Documents to secure the Term Loan Obligations; (ii) to the Intermediation Facility Secured Party pursuant
to the Intermediation Facility Documents to secure the Intermediation Facility Obligations; and/or (iii) to the Renewables Intermediation
Facility Secured Party pursuant to the Renewables Intermediation Facility Documents to secure the Renewables Intermediation Facility
Obligations; and

 

WHEREAS,
it is the desire of the parties hereto to set forth their respective rights and priorities with respect to the Collateral.

 

NOW,
THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing and the mutual covenants herein contained and other good and valuable consideration,
the existence and sufficiency of which is expressly recognized by all of the parties hereto, the parties agree as follows:

Facility Secured Parties, the percentage determined by dividing the Renewables Intermediation Facility Secured Parties respective
Renewables Intermediation Facility Obligations outstanding after the Renewables Intermediation Facility Secured Parties have marshalled,

Renewables Intermediation Facility Obligations under the respective Renewables Intermediation Facility Documents, by the sum of
such Renewables Intermediation Facility Obligations, plus the sum of the outstanding Term Loan Obligations and the outstanding
Intermediation Facility Obligations, in each case, as of the time of the occurrence of the event giving rise to the payment of
business interruption insurance; and (iii) with respect to the Term Loan Secured Parties, the difference between 100% and the
sum of percentages determined in items (i) and (ii) of this definition.
