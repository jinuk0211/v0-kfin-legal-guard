CREDIT AGREEMENT

This CREDIT AGREEMENT is entered into as of August 14, 2024 among CADENCE DESIGN SYSTEMS, INC., a Delaware corporation (the “

Borrower

”), the Lenders (defined herein) and BANK OF AMERICA, N.A., as Administrative Agent, Swing Line Lender and L/C Issuer.

Closing Date Refinancing

”).
